## 4. NASA Data & Resources Used (Copy-Paste Answer — Global Award Eligibility)

**Prompt:** *"Provide specific details about what NASA data and resources you used. To be eligible for a Global Award, you must use at least one NASA data or resource."*

**Recommended Copy-Paste Answer:**
> HORIZON OPS directly ingests, caches, and visualizes multiple authentic NASA planetary datasets and services, tracked with SHA-256 checksums and provenance metadata (`MOON_ME_DE421`, lunar radius $R = 1,737,400\,\text{m}$):
>
> 1. **NASA LRO LOLA South Polar Digital Elevation Models — PGDA Product 90 (`LDEM_80S_80MPP_ADJ.TIF` & `LDEM_80S_20MPP_ADJ.TIF`):**
>    - **Source:** Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) / Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA), NASA GSFC Planetary Geodynamics Data Archive (`https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/90`).
>    - **How Used:** Memory-mapped GeoTIFF polar stereographic ingestion (`80°S–90°S`, `7,600 × 7,600` float32 raster at `80 m/pixel`, plus `20 m/pixel` high-resolution tiles) for curvature-corrected 360° horizon ray-casting out to `150 km`, 2D touchdown slope hazard classification, Nearby Safe Haven scanning, and rover traverse trafficability & radio line-of-sight checks.
> 2. **NASA LRO LOLA 5 m/pixel Polar Site DEM & Quality Layers — PGDA Product 78 (`Site01_final_adj_5mpp_surf.tif`):**
>    - **Source:** NASA GSFC PGDA (`https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/LOLA_5mpp/Site01/`), including surface elevation (`_surf`), sample counts (`_ldec`), slope (`_slp`), height uncertainty (`_toterr`), and slope error (`_slperr`).
>    - **How Used:** Ultra-fine near-field relief (`5 m/pixel`) for Connecting Ridge (`Site 01`) horizon profiling and exported `129 × 129` (`20 m` spacing) 3D CesiumJS terrain meshes across four mapped catalog landing sites (`Connecting Ridge`, `Shackleton Rim`, `Malapert Mountain`, `Haworth Interior`).
> 3. **NASA JPL Horizons On-Line Ephemeris System (`https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/api/horizons.api`):**
>    - **Source:** NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory **DE421** ephemeris (`MOON_ME_DE421` Lunar Mean-Earth / Polar Axis topocentric observer `@301`).
>    - **How Used:** Retrieves topocentric apparent azimuth, elevation (Quantity `4`), equatorial angular diameter (Quantity `13`), and apparent range (Quantity `20`) for the **Sun (`NAIF 10`)** and **Earth (`NAIF 399`)** at `30-minute` cadence across 14-day mission windows (nominal and `+48h` launch delay runs), cached with SHA-256 verification for offline replay.
> 4. **NASA / GSFC / ASU LROC Wide Angle Camera Global Mosaic (`luna_wac_global`):**
>    - **Source:** NASA LRO Camera / ASU WMS via Lunaserv (`https://wms.im-ldi.com/`, CRS `IAU2000:30100`).
>    - **How Used:** Real optical surface albedo imagery draped across the interactive CesiumJS 3D South Polar lunar sphere.
> 5. **NASA Moon Trek (`https://trek.nasa.gov/moon/`) & LROC QuickMap (`https://quickmap.lroc.im-ldi.com/`):**
>    - **How Used:** Cross-verified South Pole topographic features and horizon sightlines; coordinate deep-links to LROC QuickMap are integrated in the dashboard.
